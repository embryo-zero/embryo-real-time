# Embryo Real-Time

Embryo Real-Time is Embryo-Zero's fork of [LiveKit](https://github.com/livekit/livekit) (80cfa6e, commit 80cfa6e). It does low-delay live video and calls.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE, NOTICE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
